## Trivia

- Kerr famously called his shot. He had announced "Project 222" four months before the record, specifically detailing his goal to run a mile in 222 seconds (3:42) in London.
- In preparation for the race, Kerr converted his bedroom into an altitude chamber, simulating ~8000 ft of elevation, and spent around 12 hours a day inside with around 9 hours of sleep.
